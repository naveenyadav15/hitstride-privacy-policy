HitStride – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what the HitStride app for Android (the "app") does with your information. The app is published by an individual developer based in India ("we", "us"). Using the app needs an account, so we do collect some personal information. It is listed below.

What we collect

Your account. Your email address and, if you add one, your display name. Firebase gives each account a user ID. If you sign in with a password, Firebase keeps it in a protected form; we never see it.

Your profile. Details you enter, such as your age, gender, height and activity level.

Your fitness and health information. Your daily step counts, your step goal and rest days, your weight entries and target weight, body measurements you enter, and the meals and calories you log.

Goal suggestions. The app suggests changes to your goals. We keep a record of each suggestion and whether you accepted or dismissed it. The suggestions are worked out inside the app from your own data. The app does not send your data to an outside AI service.

Feedback. Anything you type into the feedback form.

How you use the app. Which screens and features you use, and whether you reach your goals.

Crash reports and device identifiers. When the app crashes, it sends a crash report with technical details of the phone and the app. Google's Firebase services also create an installation ID for the app on your phone. If usage analytics is on, Firebase Analytics may also collect your device's advertising ID. We use it only to measure how the app is used, never to show you ads.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera input.

Health Connect and your phone's sensors

If you allow it, the app reads your step count and your weight from Android Health Connect. It only reads. It never writes to Health Connect. It also counts steps with your phone's step sensor and uses the Android "physical activity" permission to tell walking from riding in a vehicle. You can withdraw these permissions at any time in Android settings or Health Connect. The steps and weight the app records are saved to your account like the other fitness information above. Notification reminders are scheduled on your phone and are not sent from a server.

How we use it

  • to run the app: sign you in, show your history and keep it in sync on your devices;
  • to personalise your goals and suggestions;
  • to understand how the app is used and to fix crashes;
  • to answer feedback you send.

We do not sell your information, and the app shows no ads.

Who handles it

Your account and saved data are stored with Google's Firebase services (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics and Crashlytics), which act on our behalf. Google's servers may be outside your country. The app talks to them over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. The app's access rules let a signed-in user read and change only their own saved data. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security. Google's own privacy policy governs how Google handles data in these services. We share your information with no one else.

The app also keeps a copy of your history on your phone so it works offline.

Your choices

In the app, Profile → Privacy & data has a switch, "Share usage analytics". Turning it off stops Firebase Analytics events. It does not stop the app from saving short in-app usage summaries to your account, which are deleted with your account, and it does not stop crash reports in the released app. You can edit or remove the entries you log, and you can withdraw the Health Connect and activity permissions in Android settings.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information until you delete your account. To delete it, open the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account, and confirm with your password (or an emailed link). This deletes your account and what is saved to it: steps, weight, meals, goals and profile, body measurements, usage summaries, goal-suggestion records and feedback. It also removes anything on that phone that had not synced yet.

If you cannot open the app, you can ask us to delete your account from your email: see how to delete your HitStride account.

Crash reports and Analytics events that Google has already received are not removed by deleting your account. Google keeps them for a limited period under its own settings.

Google Play and this page

You install the app through Google Play, which handles your download under Google's own privacy policy. If you open this policy from the app, your browser loads it from GitHub Pages, which GitHub runs. GitHub may log visits, such as your IP address, under its own privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

Children

HitStride is not designed for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Privacy laws in some places, such as the EU, the UK, California and India, give people rights over their personal information, including access, correction and deletion. To use any of them, contact us as described below. You can delete your account yourself in the app at any time.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app collects something new or uses your information in a new way, we will update this policy before that version is released. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
