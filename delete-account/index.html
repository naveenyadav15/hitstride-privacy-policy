Delete your HitStride account

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This page is for the HitStride app for Android. You can delete your HitStride account and the data saved to it in the app, or ask us to do it by email if you cannot open the app.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open HitStride and go to Profile.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete account. If you sign in with an emailed link instead, tap "Email me a link instead", open the link on the same phone, and tap Delete account once more.

The deletion takes effect straight away and cannot be undone.

Option 2: by email, without the app

  1. Email hello@manramtech.com with the subject "Delete my HitStride account".
  2. Send it from the email address you signed up with. We use that to check the account is yours. We may reply with a question if we cannot match it.
  3. We delete the account and its data within 30 days of confirming it is yours, and we email you when it is done.

What is deleted

Your account (email, display name and sign-in details) and everything saved to it: daily steps, weight entries, meals and calories, goals and profile details, body measurements, in-app usage summaries, goal-suggestion records and any feedback you sent. In the app, anything on that phone that had not synced yet is removed too.

What is not deleted

Crash reports and Firebase Analytics events that Google already received are not removed by deleting your account. Google keeps them for a limited period under its own settings. The app does not attach your email address to them. Uninstalling the app removes the app's data on that phone. Data in Health Connect is managed in Health Connect.

For everything the app collects and why, read the HitStride privacy policy.
